# Writing for Tomorrow's Reader

Tomorrow's reader may be a colleague, a stranger, or your future self. In every case, they arrive without the conversation that produced the note.

## Leave the missing context behind

A durable page names its subject early, explains unfamiliar terms once, and links a claim to the evidence that supports it. These small choices reduce the work required to trust the text.

Good documentation is not a transcript of thinking. It is a path through thinking that another person can follow.
