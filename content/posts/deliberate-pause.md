# The Value of a Deliberate Pause

Speed is useful when the next action is obvious. It is less useful when a quick answer hides an unresolved question. A deliberate pause creates room to notice what the first answer assumed.

## A practical pause

Before sending an important message, ask:

- Is the decision reversible?
- What evidence would change the conclusion?
- Is silence better than a rushed reply?

The goal is not to delay everything. It is to spend attention where a mistake would be expensive and move quickly where it would not.
