# A Small Ritual for Clear Notes

A useful note does not need a complicated system. It needs a repeatable moment: capture the thought, name the context, and leave one sentence about why it mattered.

## Three lines are enough

Try ending a work session with these prompts:

1. What changed?
2. What remains uncertain?
3. What should be checked next?

This short ritual makes a note readable when its original context has disappeared. It also keeps a notebook from becoming a pile of fragments that only made sense on the day they were written.

> Write for the person who will return to this page with no memory of today.
